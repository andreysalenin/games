For parents

Cora says a sentence in Portuguese and your child taps the matching picture. Tapping a wrong picture is fine: Cora says what it is (“Não, esse é o gato.”) and asks again.

Stars

  • 3 stars: right on the first try
  • 2 stars: after one wrong tap
  • 1 star: right in the end

Hearing the sentence again (the blue speaker) never costs a star. Five questions make a level; finishing one opens the next and adds a sticker to the album.

100 levels in 10 worlds

Pictures start in a 2 × 2 grid (worlds 1 to 3), then 2 × 3 (worlds 4 to 7), then 3 × 3 (worlds 8 to 10). Words come first (farm animals, food, home, wild animals), then colours, numbers to six, and short sentences about actions, like “O menino está bebendo água.” (Brazil) or “O menino está a beber água.” (Portugal). Each level starts by showing its new words.

Changes the accent, the words and the grammar that differ. Brazil: o cachorro, o ônibus, o trem, o sorvete, está comendo, Encontre… Portugal: o cão, o autocarro, o comboio, o gelado, está a comer, Encontra…
