Momo, Lou, Pipoca and Cora

Four language games. Open one in Safari, then add it to the Home Screen so it opens like an app, full screen and without internet.

Momo a faimFrench · reading and countingMomo the monster orders food; read the order and serve it. Lou le hibouFrench · listening, for little onesLou the owl says a word or a sentence; tap the right picture. Pipoca tem fomePortuguese · reading and countingPipoca the monster orders food; read the order and serve it. Cora, a corujaPortuguese · listening, for little onesCora the owl says a word or a sentence; tap the right picture.

Put a game on the Home Screen

  1. Open the game above, in Safari.
  2. Tap ••• next to the address bar, then Share. On older iPhones the Share button is in the bottom bar.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen. If it isn't in the list, tap Edit Actions at the bottom and add it.
  4. Leave Open as Web App on and tap Add.
  5. Open the game once from its new icon while you have internet. After that it works offline.

Good to know

  • The games talk with the iPhone's own voices. For clearer ones, download an Enhanced French or Portuguese voice in Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices.
  • Each game's parents' screen picks the accent: France or Canada for Momo and Lou, Brazil or Portugal for Pipoca and Cora.
  • Progress stays on this iPhone, inside each game. Removing a game's icon from the Home Screen erases its progress.
